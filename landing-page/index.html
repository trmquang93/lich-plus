Features

Everything you need in a Vietnamese calendar

Combining centuries of Vietnamese tradition with modern calendar functionality

Vietnamese Lunar Calendar

Accurate Can-Chi calculations with the traditional 12 Truc system. View both solar and lunar dates at a glance.

Astrology Integration

Hoang Dao (auspicious hours), good and bad stars, and day quality assessments based on traditional Vietnamese astrology.

Multi-Calendar Sync

Seamlessly sync with Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, Microsoft Outlook, and ICS feeds. All your events in one place.

Smart Events

Create events with lunar-based recurrence. Perfect for anniversaries, death anniversaries (gio), and traditional celebrations.

Day Quality

Know if today is auspicious for important activities. Get recommendations based on traditional Vietnamese almanac wisdom.

Bilingual Support

Full Vietnamese and English localization. Switch languages anytime to suit your preference.
