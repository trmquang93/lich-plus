Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Introduction

Welcome to Lich Viet ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience when using our Vietnamese lunar calendar application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Calendar Events: Event titles, descriptions, dates, times, and recurrence settings you create within the app
  • Tasks: Task details, due dates, categories, and completion status
  • Preferences: App settings, language preferences, and notification configurations

Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, and unique device identifiers
  • Usage Data: Features used, app interactions, and crash reports

Calendar Sync & Third-Party Services

Lich Viet offers integration with external calendar services. When you enable these integrations:

Apple Calendar

When syncing with Apple Calendar, we access your calendar data through Apple's EventKit framework. This data remains on your device and syncs through Apple's iCloud infrastructure according to Apple's privacy policies.

Google Calendar

If you connect Google Calendar, we use Google's Calendar API to read and write calendar events. We access only the calendars you explicitly authorize. Google's privacy policy governs how Google handles your data.

Microsoft Outlook Calendar

Microsoft Calendar integration uses Microsoft Graph API. We access only authorized calendar data. Microsoft's privacy policy applies to data processed by Microsoft.

ICS Calendar Feeds

ICS feeds are read-only subscriptions. We fetch and parse calendar data from URLs you provide but do not modify the source calendars.

How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the app's calendar and task management features
  • Sync your events across connected calendar services
  • Calculate and display Vietnamese lunar calendar dates and astrological information
  • Send notifications and reminders you configure
  • Improve app performance and fix bugs
  • Develop new features based on usage patterns

Data Storage & Security

Your calendar and task data is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. Data synchronized with third-party calendar services is transmitted securely using HTTPS encryption.

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information only:

  • With calendar services you explicitly connect (Google, Microsoft, Apple)
  • When required by law or to protect our legal rights
  • With service providers who assist in app operations under strict confidentiality agreements

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Delete your data by uninstalling the app or disconnecting synced services
  • Revoke Access: Disconnect third-party calendar integrations at any time through the app's settings

Children's Privacy

Lich Viet is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
